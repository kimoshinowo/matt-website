Professional Skills

Electrochemistry

I have experience in the preparion of materials for, assembly and charcterisation of energy storage devices such as supercapacitors, lithium-ion batteries and primary/secondary aqueous batteries, each with liquid and solid state electrolytes. I am familiar with most of the common electrochemical techniques used in this field, such as impedance spectroscopy (EIS), cyclic and linear voltammetry, chronoamperometry/potentiometry, and galvanostatic charge-discharge (GCD) experiments for assessment of cycling performance.

Organic Synthesis

I have a strong background in organic synthesis due my prior research which required me to synthesise novel monomers for bio-based polymers and novel ligands for photocatalysts. As a result, I am well-experiened in the majority of techniques used in organic synthesis, including advanced separation techniques such as column chromatography, and the manipulation of air-sensitive or hazardous substances under inert atmospheres. Naturally, I have a lot of experience in the common characterisation techniquues for small molecules, such as NMR (1D & 2D), FTIR, XRD and mass spectometry.

Polymerisation

I have experience in synthesising polymers from monomers and resins using most of the common polymerisation techniques including ring-opening polymerisation (ROP), metathesis (incl. ROMP & ADMET), polycondensation, thiol-ene reactions, and many more. Polymer classes which I have experience in include: polycarbonates & polyesters, polyethers & polythioethers, polyurethanes & polyureas, polyamic acids & polyimides, epoxies. Naturally, I am well-experienced in a plethora of solid- and liquid-state material characterisation techniques such as NMR (1D & 2D), GPC, DSC, TGA, FTIR, Raman, XPS, MALDI-TOF, viscometry, tensile strength analysis, etc.

Polymer Functionalisation

The modification of polymers is also an important skill in which I am well-versed. I am experienced in the chemical modification of polymers to alter the their properties and characteristics by conversion of functional groups within the polymer backbone, various forms of crosslinking, chain-extension, etc.

Python

Experience in Python is a relatively new skill which I have been developing, in part to support my lab-based data analysis, but also simply because I find learning to code a challenge which I really enjoy solving. So far, I have used Python to develop some basic scripts for battery cycling experiments and thermal analysis data.
