About me

My main areas of expertise are in the fields of polymer chemistry, electrochemistry and material science. See below to find out more about my industrial and academic experiences where I gained this expertise, or visit the "Skills" page for more details about my relevant skills.

Career

iGii - Development Scientist (2024 to Present)

At iGii, I work as a Development Scientist in the Advanced Materials department where I'm responsible for the innovation and development of materials for commercial graphene-based energy storage devices. This includes developing new forms of and substrates for our pure 3D carbon foam, as well as new types of electrolytes.

Sika - Research And Development Chemist (2023)

My role at Sika invovled the development of novel polymer coating systems for waterproofing, roofing and flooring applications. My areas of expertise included two-component polyurethanes, water-based polyurethane dispersions and water-based epoxy coatings. As part of these developments, I sourced and worked towards the incorporation of bio-based raw materials in alignment with Sika's journey towards Net-Zero.

Industrial Chemicals Ltd - Analytical Chemist (2017-2019)

I was responsible for the routine analysis of samples of chemicals made on the plant, including sodium hypochlorite, caustic soda, HCl, ferric sulphate and amine oxides. Sample analysis was conducted via wet chemistry methods and various analytical instrumentation including ICP-OES, GC and IC. I also investigated ongoing issues on the plant and developed new testing methods.

Education

PhD - University of Bath (2019-2023)

My research explored the synthesis, modification and characterisation of novel monomers and polymers from bio-derived feedstocks to be used in the preparation and study of solid polymer electrolyte films for application in lithium-ion batteries.

Thesis title: Synthesis and Characterisation of Novel Sugar-Derived Solid Polymer Electrolytes for Lithium-Ion Batteries.

Publications:

  • Xylose- and Nucleoside-Based Polymers via Thiol-ene Polymerization toward Sugar-Derived Solid Polymer Electrolytes, ACS Applied Polymer Materials, 2024, 6, 3, 1622-1632.
  • Crosslinked Xylose-Based Polyester as a Bio-Derived and Degradable Solid Polymer Electrolyte for Li+-Ion Conduction, Journal of Materials Chemistry - A, 2022, 10, 6796-6808.
  • Cross-Linking of Sugar-Derived Polyethers and Boronic Acids for Renewable, Self-Healing, and Single-Ion Conducting Organogel Polymer Electrolytes, ACS Applied Energy Materials, 2023, 6, 5, 2924-2935.

MChem - University of York (2013-2017)

I completed a Masters degree in Chemistry at the University of York. As part of this degree, I completed a paid 8-month research internship at the Université Grenoble Alpes, France, in the synthesis and study of molecular photocatalytic systems for hydrogen production via the proton reduction in the water-splitting electrochemical reaction.

Publication: Challenging the [Ru(bpy)3]2+ Photosensitizer with a Triazatriangulenium Robust Organic Dye for Visible-Light-Driven Hydrogen Production in Water, ACS Catalysis, 2018, 8, 5, 3792-3802.
